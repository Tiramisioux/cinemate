## Welcome screen
<!-- key: pane.welcome -->

What operators see on the HDMI output for a moment after the camera boots.

### Show welcome message
<!-- key: card.system.welcome.show -->

Displays a greeting over the image for a few seconds after boot, before the live view takes over.

### Message text
<!-- key: card.system.welcome.message -->

The exact words shown on screen. Keep it short — it sits over the image, not on black.

### Welcome image
<!-- key: card.system.welcome.image -->

Optional logo or splash image shown over the message, for a moment after boot. PNG only.

## Wi‑Fi hotspot
<!-- key: pane.wifi -->

The network the camera broadcasts so you can reach this page from a phone or laptop.

### Broadcast hotspot
<!-- key: card.system.wifi_hotspot.enabled -->

Turns the camera into its own Wi‑Fi access point. Turning this off means you'll need a wired connection to reach it again.

### Network name
<!-- key: card.system.wifi_hotspot.name -->

The SSID that shows up in Wi‑Fi lists. Useful to rename once you're running more than one unit.

### Password
<!-- key: card.system.wifi_hotspot.password -->

At least 8 characters. Anyone with this can join the hotspot and open this settings page.
