## The recommendation

**Reject D and E. Adopt C, reached through B. Fix F-204 before either.**

Four steps, each shippable and revertible: fix the unguarded event dispatch (~10 lines);
generate the design tokens from one source and gate `design_token_diff.py --strict` on the
same commit; lift the section spec out of `simple_gui.py` into data; generalise the layout
primitives region by region behind a flag. Region anchors stay per-surface — a 1920
instrument panel and a phone browser should not share a grid. Surface 4 is excluded
permanently.

## The finding that decided it

**F-238.** I went looking for how absolute the HDMI layout really is, because F-008 —
a seed finding carried unqualified through seven sessions — says it is absolute
1920-reference pixel constants, and that is the whole case for the options being far apart.

It is only half true. `_top_row_layout` measures each of the six top-row groups' rendered
text width, sums them, and distributes the free space between two anchors as an equal gap.
That is a justified flex row — `justify-content: space-between` — implemented in PIL, with
a conditional HDR badge participating in the flow. `draw_left_sections` walks the section
spec, honours each section's `condition` predicate, centres labels on measured width, and
advances a cursor, with gaps that adapt to whether the VU bar is present.

**The HDMI GUI is a fixed grid of regions with content-driven flow inside the two busiest
ones.** What is pinned is the region anchors and the bottom status row — which, on a
fixed-resolution instrument panel, is a reasonable thing to pin.

So KICKOFF §7's central tension — "immediate-mode raster vs retained-mode declarative
layout" — **overstates the gap** (F-239). Option C is not "port CSS to PIL". It is
generalising two primitives that already exist to the regions that do not yet use them.

Combined with S07's F-203 (the state model is already shared) and F-215 (the widget spec
already exists), all three of option C's hard parts are partly built.

## The correction to KICKOFF

KICKOFF §7 constraint 1 says DRM exclusivity is *"likely fatal to D and E"* — and asks for
it to be proven from source rather than assumed. Doing that produced a split verdict:
**right for D, wrong for E.** Option E rasterises HTML to a bitmap and writes `/dev/fb0`,
exactly as `simple_gui` does today; it introduces no second DRM client at all. E is fatal on
C3 and C4 — no HTML rasteriser reaches 12 fps full-screen on a CM5, and the project's own
doc already warns that the far cheaper PIL path should not exceed 15 — but not on C1.

Getting this right matters because "DRM kills it" is a load-bearing argument, and applying
it to the wrong option would have made the ADR wrong for the right reason.

## What the ADR refuses to decide

**C2 stays open.** PI-009 is unanswered and the ADR says so in three places. S07/S08
narrowed it three times — F-223 (the preview binds its display at process start and cannot
rebind, which is why HDMI hot-plug triggers a full capture restart), F-227 (`dev`'s
`drm_preview.cpp` already enumerates DRM planes and programs a spare one, degrading
gracefully when none is free), F-229 (both repos describe `--same-hdmi` as preview and GUI
sharing one output) — and PI-009 now asks for the answerable form: **how many overlay planes
are free on the primary CRTC, with `--same-hdmi` on and off.**

C2 does not discriminate between A, B and C, which is why the ADR can be proposed without
it. It would matter to anyone reviving D or E.

**C3 and C6 are argued, not measured**, and the ADR labels both `probable`. → PI-016.

## Corrections made during the session

- **`ZOOM_HIGHLIGHT_COLOR` was nearly reported as a dangling annotation.** The first version
  of `design_token_diff.py` read only module-level constants; the constant is a
  function-local at `simple_gui.py:1226`. It would have shipped a false drift finding. The
  script now walks the whole tree and the near-miss is in its docstring.
- **The same script initially mismatched `--lock`** because `self.colors["lock"]` is an
  RGBA 4-tuple and the matcher required 3. Fixed to compare on RGB.
- **F-007's framing was too generous to the code.** It says the colours are "synced only by
  a comment". Only 3 of 16 have a comment; 11 have no stated link at all (F-232). The
  finding understated the problem, which is the opposite of this review's usual error.
- **I did not re-derive F-008; I contradicted it.** F-238 is recorded as a correction with
  its own citations rather than an amendment, because F-008 is a KICKOFF seed finding and
  the ledger convention is that corrections are additive and traceable.

## Method note

No subagents. The work was three targeted reads (`_top_row_layout`, `draw_left_sections`,
`setup_resources`), one AST measurement, and one new checker — the ADR's weight comes from
`GUI-INVENTORY.md` and `GUI-STATE-MODEL.md`, which were already on disk. Writing the
checker before writing the ADR section that depends on it caught two of the four corrections
above.
